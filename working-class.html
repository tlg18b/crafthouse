Working Class 2027

Drama · 69 min

Logline

When a reclusive architect is visited by his estranged, working-class family, he reveals a hidden secret: a wish-granting fairy. As old tensions resurface, the family must decide if it is a blessing, a curse, or a mirror to their own dysfunctions.
