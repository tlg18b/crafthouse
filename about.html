THE FOUNDER

Jaelyn Ellis is a New York-based writer, director, and producer dedicated to championing character-driven stories from underrepresented voices with nuance, depth, and humanity beyond trauma.

Through Craft House Pictures, she supports emerging filmmakers and produces acclaimed work that has screened at Palm Springs ShortFest, HollyShorts, and NewFest. Her film Girlhood earned support from The Future of Film is Female and was featured by NoBudge and the Museum of Modern Art (MoMA).

A graduate of Florida State University's College of Motion Picture Arts, Jaelyn brings extensive physical production experience as a Line Producer and Unit Production Manager overseeing six-figure independent feature films. She is a 2026 WAVE Grant recipient and a 2026 Sundance Producers Lab Fellow for her sophomore feature, Likeness, and continues to develop original work across writing and directing.
